### Acquisition descriptors (focus, black level, noise) - leakage control
<a id="acquisition_sharpness"></a>

`acquisition_sharpness` · acquisition · rejected · owner: luca

**What:** How each spot was imaged, on the shared harmonisation crop. Gate columns: BSE black level, BSE Immerkaer noise (graphite units, before top-up), Inlens saturation, SE curtain index (stripe-line vs flank power in a Welch spectrum of the rank-normalised SE) and focus (Laplacian variance on graphite interiors minus the white-noise term). Diagnostics: graphite level, BSE clip-at-0 fraction, interior CNR, Inlens rank noise. gate_status() compares a new spot with the frozen known-session envelope and perturbation-tested range (G-A / G-B / G-C). Not material features: never classifier inputs.

**Why it matters for the battery:** None directly; Gate 1 of the verdict and the negative controls. Protects every material KPI from mistaking a microscope change (session black level, noise, focus, curtaining) for a material change, and exposes the image-height / black-level leakage trap in the Polaron notes.

**Findings on our data:** All columns session-locked (session R2 0.80-1.00) with no within-mixed-session batch effect (exact p 0.51-0.83; thin hints: Inlens saturation p 0.094, B1 lower in 3/3 sessions; focus p 0.062, B2 2-6 % lower). Curtain index flags only session 2316 (1.64-1.68 vs 0.89-1.16, z +8.8/+9.3); clip0 tracks porosity (r +0.63 within session) so it is diagnostic only. Gate 1 (frozen limits, curtain envelope without 2316): 29/31 G-A, the two 2316 spots G-C on curtain index. Acquisition-only LR on these 9 columns: balanced accuracy 0.58 leave-one-site-out, 0.49 leave-one-session-out (chance 0.33, perm p 0.03/0.04) [features/acquisition README, 2026-10-03, 31 spots]. Moved 2026-10-03 out of the features into preprocessing step 5 (preprocessing/acquisition.py): it is not a material feature, so it no longer writes to features.csv; process_data.py prints a WARNING per spot outside the known range and writes processed/acquisition.csv. Gate 1 of the verdict checks only pixel size and detector set.

### Fines: share of solid in small detached fragments
<a id="calendering_fines"></a>

`calendering_fines` · morphology · idea

**What:** Solid (graphite) connected components with ECD < 1 um that touch no larger particle (1-px dilation test), as a share of solid area and number per 100 um2; Inlens used to exclude carbon-black/binder blobs (bright fine texture). ### Two-level graphite domains (blend vs channelling)
<a id="carbon_domains"></a>

`carbon_domains` · composition · v1_only · first repo: `features/carbon_domains` · owner: luca

First-repo columns: `carbon_dark_domain_frac`

**What:** Share of the graphite interior in the darker of two BSE graphite grey levels (drift-corrected 0.5 um level map; 2-component Gaussian mixture at and below the main graphite peak, >= 6 % contrast gate; patches >= 25 um2), plus the share of dark/main interfaces lying on particle boundaries (void/bright edges or Inlens ridges): whole-particle contrast suggests a blend, contrast inside particles suggests electron channelling. Differs from carbon_binder_domain: that is the binder/carbon-black web between particles, this is two grades of the active graphite itself.

**Why it matters for the battery:** The natural/synthetic graphite blend ratio is a hidden supplier change. It moves first-cycle efficiency through surface area (Winter 1998) and slurry extensional rheology (JCIS 2024), and natural graphite is weaker at fast and low-temperature charging (Adv. Mater. 2022). Capacity, cycle-life and swelling differences are general knowledge with no verified citation. A CoA reports neither the ratio nor how the grades are mixed.

**Findings on our data:** Final integrated run: dark fraction B1 0.013 / B2 0.082 / B3 0.028 (domains in 4/7, 3/7, 2/17 spots), sep 0.46, q 0.33, within-session p 0.31 (192 relabellings), session R2 0.63, perturbation ratio 0.24. The session-adjusted B1 -1.70 SD comes from session 2080 alone (-0.21 SD without it); B2 is -0.79 SD (-0.49 without 2080). Boundary share sep 1.61, q 0.075 (cross-spot shuffle, ignores session), but within-session p 1.00 (session 2080 only), perturbation ratio 1.00 (fails 0.5), and the excess over chance is similar in all batches (+0.23 / +0.21 / +0.22, n 4/3/2; permutation p 0.80; inside 2080 +0.19 / +0.24 / +0.19). B1's higher raw share is its higher chance level. Battery reading: no sign of a graphite blend or grade change in B1 or B2 (low confidence). The two session-2316 B1 spots (Si-phase lead, n_eff = 1) read 0; a full switch to one other grade would also read 0. A shift would be neither good nor bad, only a sign the supplier's graphite recipe may have changed (ICE and capacity direction unknown until the grades are identified). A high boundary share can also come from pore-back. Tier 3 diagnostic; compare within session only.

### Two-point correlation function S2(r) of void, solid and bright phase, direction-resolved
<a id="two_point_correlation"></a>

`two_point_correlation` · spatial_statistics · v1_only · first repo: `features/two_point_correlation` · owner: luca

First-repo columns: `s2_void_corrlen_x_um`, `s2_void_corrlen_y_um`, `s2_void_integral_range_um2`, `s2_bright_corrlen_x_um`, `s2_bright_corrlen_y_um`, `s2_bright_integral_range_um2`

**What:** S2 of the shared harmonised void, solid (= not void) and bright masks along rows (x) and columns (y): zero-padded FFT autocorrelation per line, normalised by overlap counts. **Why it matters for the battery:** Pore size scale and calendering anisotropy without particle segmentation (pore flattening raises through-plane tortuosity); bright-phase correlation length tracks Si particle size / clustering (swelling hot-spots); the integral range sets the single-image error bar of porosity and Si fraction.

**Findings on our data:** Final run (rank_features.py + robustness.py --perturb, 31 spots): no detectable batch difference in any S2 column (not proven equivalence; only 3 B1 and 5 B2 sites sit in mixed sessions). sep 0.05-0.35, q >= 0.62; within-session p 0.37-0.88 except bright x 0.052 / y 0.13 (B1 below the other lot in all 3 shared sessions, -0.9 / -1.5 SD; does not survive 8 columns, split-half ~0; if real, finer not clustered Si); perturbation ratio <= 0.23 (void), <= 0.14 (bright; the builder's 0.81 is superseded). B1's raw void-length excess (+0.3 / +0.4 SD) is session 2316 only (4ih2ggld z +2.5 / +3.2; n_eff = 1, invisible to the within-session test); without it B1 is -0.1 / -0.3 SD, and within shared sessions -0.8 / -0.9 SD. Battery reading: no sign of a calendering, wetting or Si-dispersion change (pore x/y 1.54 / 1.63 / 1.53, within-session p 0.56). Not in the v1 tiers. The void lengths track Tier-2 pore_lt_d50_um (rho 0.89-0.92), so in v2 they belong as its identity companion, not an extra vote. s2_solid_corrlen_y_um, the identity companion of Tier-1 solid_chord_y_um, is the pore scale (r -0.07 with the chord), so that check is uninformative and could confirm for the wrong reason (high at 2316 because the pores are larger).
